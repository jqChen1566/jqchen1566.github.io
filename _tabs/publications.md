Yishi Li, **Jianqi Chen**, Xuyu Qin, Juan Li. Mechanistic origin of TBAI-controlled regioselectivity reversal in the palladium-catalyzed hydrocarboxylation of terminal aryl alkynes with oxalic acid. *Organic Chemistry Frontiers*, 2026. DOI: [10.1039/d6qo01302c](https://doi.org/10.1039/d6qo01302c)
